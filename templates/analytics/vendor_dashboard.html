How these numbers are calculated

  • Gross sales: price × quantity of your items that are not cancelled.
  • Recognised sales: the same, limited to orders whose payment has been received.
  • Net revenue: recognised sales minus the commission rate stored on each item at purchase.
  • Coupons are funded by CoreCart and do not reduce your earnings.
