About CoreCart

CoreCart is a multi-vendor marketplace. Independent businesses apply to sell, our team reviews each application, and approved sellers list their products for customers to discover.

How payment works

CoreCart currently supports cash on delivery and bank transfer. Orders are confirmed immediately, but are only marked as paid once payment has actually been received and recorded by our team.

Need help?

Signed-in customers can open a support ticket from their account. Our team responds to every ticket.
